Her story

Moves as an Air Force family

Danielle was born in Virginia and spent her childhood on the East Coast before her family moved to Olympia, Washington, when her dad retired from the Air Force.

Moving would continue to be a theme throughout her life. She married her college sweetheart, a newly commissioned Air Force officer, and over his career they lived throughout the country and moved 12 times. Danielle became all too familiar with the process of moving and relocation — and that experience shapes how she guides every client today.

Before real estate, Danielle spent several years in non-profit development and marketing. She now uses those skills to help clients buy, sell and invest in property. Her compassionate heart keeps her involved in serving the underprivileged in her community, through volunteer work and as a board member of a local non-profit.

Multiple moves aside, Danielle has always been passionate about travel and has explored much of Europe. She loves the thrill of adventure while also having a deep appreciation for settling down. When she isn't busy with real estate or serving the community, you'll find her skiing, discovering new restaurants and coffee shops, or enjoying time with her family. Her charisma is infectious and overflows into every part of her life.
