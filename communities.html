Utah's most sought-after communities.

An insider's introduction to the places Danielle's clients call home — the lifestyle, the commute and what makes each one special.

No. 01

Salt Lake City

The Avenues · Sugar House · Federal Heights · East Bench

Utah's capital pairs historic tree-lined neighborhoods with a growing downtown, a major international airport and the state's largest academic medical campus at the University of Utah — all with mountain canyons just minutes from the city.

Lifestyle
Walkable historic districts, dining, arts and professional sports
Outdoors
Red Butte, City Creek and Emigration canyons close by
Good to know
Close to University of Utah Health, Huntsman Cancer Institute and the VA
No. 02

Holladay & Cottonwood Heights

Mature trees · Larger lots · Canyon access

Gracious, established neighborhoods on Salt Lake's east side. Holladay is known for mature landscaping and larger lots; Cottonwood Heights sits at the mouths of Big and Little Cottonwood Canyons.

Lifestyle
Quiet streets, a village-style city center and easy freeway access
Outdoors
Brighton, Solitude, Alta and Snowbird up the Cottonwood canyons
Good to know
Convenient to Intermountain Medical Center in Murray
No. 03

Park City & Summit County

Old Town · Deer Valley · Kimball Junction · Jeremy Ranch

A historic mining town turned world-class mountain resort. Park City offers two major ski areas, a lively Main Street and year-round trails, a short drive from Salt Lake via Parley's Canyon.

Lifestyle
Main Street dining, galleries and a true four-season mountain town
Outdoors
Deer Valley and Park City Mountain, plus hundreds of miles of trails
Good to know
Roughly 35–45 minutes to Salt Lake City International, depending on traffic
No. 04

Heber Valley & Midway

Midway · Heber City · Jordanelle

Wide pastoral valleys framed by the Wasatch. Midway is known for its Swiss-inspired character, while Heber City offers more space and newer neighborhoods — just over the ridge from Park City.

Lifestyle
Small-town pace, open views and a growing local dining scene
Outdoors
Jordanelle and Deer Creek reservoirs, Soldier Hollow and Wasatch Mountain State Park
Good to know
About 20 minutes to Park City; under an hour to Salt Lake in most conditions
No. 05

Draper & the South Valley

Draper · Sandy · South Jordan

Newer homes, strong amenities and some of the valley's best trail systems, with an easy commute north to Salt Lake or south to Utah County.

Lifestyle
Family-friendly neighborhoods, newer construction and shopping close by
Outdoors
Corner Canyon's trail network and quick access to the Cottonwood canyons
Good to know
Central to both Salt Lake and Utah County employers
No. 06

Lehi & Utah County

Silicon Slopes · Provo · Orem

The heart of Utah's tech corridor. Lehi and its neighbors offer abundant new construction and a younger community, with BYU and Utah Valley University to the south.

Lifestyle
Thanksgiving Point, new town centers and a fast-growing restaurant scene
Outdoors
American Fork Canyon, Utah Lake and Provo Canyon nearby
Good to know
Home to Intermountain Primary Children's Hospital's Lehi campus
