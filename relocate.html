The process

Six steps to home.

Every move is different, but the best ones share a clear plan. Here's how Danielle guides you from wherever you are today to settled in Utah.

01

Discovery call

We talk through your move — timeline, budget, commute, schools, lifestyle — and what home needs to feel like for your family.

02

Your Utah shortlist

Danielle narrows the map to the communities that fit, with honest notes on the trade-offs of each one.

03

Tour — in person or virtual

A personalized area tour when you're in town, or live video walkthroughs of homes and neighborhoods when you can't be.

04

Financing & strategy

Introductions to trusted lenders, including those who understand physician loans, plus a clear plan for renting first or buying now.

05

Offer to closing

Strategic negotiation and steady communication through inspection, appraisal and closing — coordinated around your schedule.

06

Settling in

Utilities, local services and recommendations from people who live here. Danielle's support doesn't stop at the keys.

Plan Your Move
Two paths

Rent first, or buy now?

There's no single right answer — Danielle will help you weigh it honestly for your situation.

A

Rent first

Live in Utah for a season, learn the commutes and neighborhoods first-hand, then buy with total confidence. Ideal when your timeline is tight or the area is brand new to you.

B

Buy now

Move once and start building equity on day one. With virtual tours and a trusted local advocate, many clients buy before they ever arrive.

C

Sell where you are

Need to sell before you move? Danielle can coordinate with a trusted agent in your current city so both sides of the move stay in sync.
